About this app

How it works, what it does with your camera, and what it can't do.

What it does

MOSAIC-X helps learners practise a respectful social-cue process: notice what happened, consider multiple explanations, and ask rather than assume. Expression recognition is an optional demonstration—not the source of truth.

An EfficientNetB0 model can suggest which of seven visible expression categories appears in a picture. Its output is treated as uncertain evidence. A person's own words always have priority.

Who it is for

It is a portfolio prototype for learners, families and educators to explore communication strategies together. It has not been evaluated with autistic users, so it makes no claim that it improves real-world outcomes.

Privacy

  • Camera pictures go only to this app's server, which normally runs on this computer. Each one is used for a single guess and then thrown away: never saved, never emailed.
  • Songs you pick stay in your browser and are not uploaded.
  • Progress is saved only in this browser. You can download or erase it on the Progress page.

Limits: please read

  • It still makes mistakes. V3 is right on 62.1% of the 298-image target benchmark across seven expression categories. This improves the original 49.7% model, but is not reliable enough to grade or diagnose anyone.
  • Confidence is calibrated, not certainty. Temperature scaling and a 42.5% threshold were selected on validation data. On the benchmark, accepted predictions reached 69.7% accuracy at 73.2% coverage.
  • The benchmark is now developmental. Results from multiple versions have been inspected, so a new external test set is needed before presenting V3 as a final unbiased estimate.
  • A face is not a feeling. People, and especially many autistic people, may feel something without showing the “typical” expression. The app only reads expressions, never what someone really feels.
  • Not a medical tool. It does not diagnose, assess or measure autism or any condition.
  • Training data is limited. About 2,000 photos from two public datasets, not balanced across ages or skin tones, so it may work worse for some people.
